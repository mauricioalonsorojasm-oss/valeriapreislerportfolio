About

Valeria Preisler

With a background in Visual Arts and Photography, I began my career in 2013 as a Jr. Art Director, which allowed me to quickly move into Film and Photo productions, with a strong focus on the Fashion industry and Automotive. Since then, I have collaborated with clients, filmmakers, photographers and post producers on a wide range of creative projects across South America, Europe, Asia and Africa. I can develop and execute successful campaigns from start to finish — from briefings, research, and creative proposals to budget management, scheduling, team coordination, shooting, post production and final delivery. If you find my portfolio interesting, let’s talk!
